# Upgrading to v0.30.0-beta.1

This is an opt-in beta prerelease. Keep a recoverable pre-upgrade backup.

1. Stop every kei writer, including containers, scheduled jobs and services. An already-open older connection is not protected by the new-open schema check.
2. Take a matched cold backup of configuration, account state, SQLite databases and their companion files, and media. Preserve the original paths and permissions.
3. Install the beta and start one writer. The database upgrades to schema 44; older binaries cannot open it. Mixed-version operation and in-place downgrade are unsupported.
4. Inspect status and reports after the first cycle. Held identity, metadata or checkpoint obligations do not establish a complete backup, even when operational health is healthy.

To roll back, stop all writers and restore the complete matched pre-upgrade snapshot before using the older binary. Do not point an older binary at upgraded state or combine old state with a changed media tree.

## Behavior and compatibility

Recorded paths, reservations and layout journals retain their destinations. Naming changes affect new downloads. Different provider assets are not merged merely because their bytes match; an unproved match can produce a separate download.

Cross-namespace no-redownload migration remains unsupported without supported identity and a separately authorized migration. Ambiguous historical metadata repair is unsupported; uncertain ownership and retained debt remain preserved. Do not delete state or media to make held work disappear.

The beta removes `kei install` and `kei uninstall`. Configure registration through your host supervisor; `kei service run` and `kei service status` remain available. Support export captures current state and does not read or recreate historical support-history files.

Synthetic recovery checks and isolated-account tests do not certify every reporter outcome, NFS deployment, historical repair or production service deployment. Qualification results must identify the exact commit and their fixture/platform scope.
